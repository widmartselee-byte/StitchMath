Stitch Math — Privacy

Last changed: November 2026

The short version

The app has no networking code, so there is no way for anything you type to leave your iPhone.

What stays on the device

Saved plans — the swatch counts, finished measurements, yarn details and the results worked out from them — are written to a single file inside the app's own container. They are not uploaded, not synced, and not shared with us or anyone else.

What the app never does

  • No account, sign-in or profile
  • No analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDKs
  • No camera, photo library, contacts, location, health or notification access
  • No tracking request, because there is nothing to track
  • No third-party libraries at all — everything is Apple's SwiftUI

Deleting your plans

Swipe a row in the saved-plans sheet to delete one plan, or delete the app to remove all of them. Nothing is kept anywhere else, so there is nothing for us to erase on your behalf.

Children

A workshop calculator for fabric measurements. It is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

If a future version ever stores something new, this page will describe it first and keep the "no account required" promise.
