The four steps

1. Gauge — count stitches and rows in your swatch. 2. Size — the finished width and height, plus any pattern repeat. 3. Yarn — the weight and what one skein holds. 4. Result — cast-on, rows, yarn, skeins, and a save button.

Common questions

Should I measure my swatch before or after washing?

After. Wet-block it, let it dry flat, then count. Most yarn relaxes and grows, and a swatch measured straight off the needles will make the finished piece too small.

Why is the cast-on bigger than width × stitches?

Because of the pattern repeat. If a repeat is 8 stitches with 2 edge stitches, the cast-on has to be a multiple of 8 plus 2. Step 2 shows both numbers: the straight calculation and the rounded one, with how many stitches the rounding added.

Where does the yarn estimate come from?

From the number of stitches you will work and the size of each stitch, multiplied by the safety margin you choose. It targets plain stockinette. Cables, lace, colourwork and ribbing all consume more, so keep the margin at 10 % or raise it to 20 % for those.

How accurate is it?

Close enough to buy yarn, not close enough to guarantee the last row. Two people with the same gauge can still differ by a skein through tension and tails. Buying the extra skein and keeping the receipt is the honest advice.

Centimetres or inches?

Both. Switch in ••• → About → Settings; every measurement follows. Gauge is shown per 10 cm or per 4 in depending on what your swatch was measured across.

Where are saved plans kept?

In a single file inside the app's own storage on this iPhone. No account, no sync, no cloud. Deleting the app deletes them.

Does it need a connection?

No. There is no networking code in the app at all.
